01 The decision

Two paths,
one seat.

I walked away from 225 days of building a growing YouTube channel. Not because I failed, but because I couldn't commit to two paths at once.

I was working on it day and night. The burnout of constantly splitting my focus between content and sim racing made the choice clear. So I stopped, and went all in on this.

02 Canadian Grand Prix · Montreal 2026

Racing felt
real again.

This year I went to the Canadian Grand Prix in Montreal, and it reignited everything. I got caps signed by two Ferrari Driver Academy drivers, and by Alba Larsen from Ferrari's F1 Academy team. Being around that environment made racing feel real again, not just a fantasy on a screen.
