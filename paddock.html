01 The decision

Two paths,
one seat.

I walked away from 225 days of building a growing YouTube channel. Not because I failed, but because I couldn't commit to two paths at once.

For 225 days I worked on that channel day and night, then jumped into the rig with whatever was left. The burnout of constantly splitting my focus between content and sim racing made the choice clear.

So I stopped, and went all in on this: the rig, the kart track and the Road to Racing. One path instead of two.

I said it in Road to Racing · Episode 01 ↗

02 Canadian Grand Prix · Montreal 2026

Racing felt
real again.

This year I went to the Canadian Grand Prix in Montreal, and it reignited everything. I got caps signed by Dino Beganovic from the Ferrari Driver Academy and by Alba Larsen from Ferrari's F1 Academy team, and Sebastián Montoya signed for me in the Prema paddock.

Being around that environment made racing feel real again, not just a fantasy on a screen. The garages, the cars, the drivers walking past with a pen in their hand and a session to get back to: all the things I'd only ever seen through a monitor were right there.
